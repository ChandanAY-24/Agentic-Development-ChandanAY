# 04 — Group 4 Teams (Documentation, Business & Data Analyst, Marketing)

## Team 11 — Documentation Team

**Scope:** Executive, technical, and product documentation synchronized with the codebase.

- **Deliverables:**
  - `docs/PRD` (Product Requirements Document), `docs/features/`, `README.md`, `LICENSE`.
  - **PPT / Pitch & Architecture Decks** (structured slide outlines + Mermaid or `diagram-design` editorial HTML+SVG diagrams for stakeholders/clients).
  - **User & Operator Docs:** Onboarding guides, API references, runbooks, and release handoff reports (`/handoff-ca-agent`).

---

## Team 12 — Business and Data Analyst Team

**Scope:** Commercial viability, unit economics, cost transparency, and growth analytics.

- **Analysis Pillars:**
  - **Business Value & Logistics:** Operational workflows, fulfillment/delivery (E-commerce), SLA/support (SaaS), compute/GPU/API unit economics (AI/CV/ML).
  - **Financial Plan & Cost Optimization:** Revenue generation models, expense tracking, cloud/token cost optimization, **Added Cost** & **Hidden Cost** discovery (payment fees, egress, third-party APIs, compliance).
  - **Business Risk Management:** Market, regulatory, vendor lock-in, and churn risks with mitigations.
  - **Sales & Data Strategy:** Funnel metrics, cohort retention, pricing tiers, and KPI dashboards reported to the Developer.

### Sub-Agent: Finance Management Agent (`Finance Agent`)

**Scope:** End-to-end financial governance, budgeting, cashflow/runway forecasting, unit economics, and payment/tax readiness across the project lifecycle.

- **Core Responsibilities:**
  - **Budgeting, Cashflow & Runway:** CapEx/OpEx forecasting, monthly burn rate, runway modeling, and milestone-based budget allocation.
  - **Unit Economics & Pricing:** CAC, LTV, payback period, gross/net margin analysis, break-even modeling, and SaaS/E-commerce pricing tier validation.
  - **Added & Hidden Cost Governance:** Audit payment gateway fees, cloud/egress spend, LLM/API token budgets, third-party licenses, and compliance overhead.
  - **Payment Schedules & Tax Readiness:** Multi-stage payment breakdowns (aligned with [05-brand-marketing-proposals.md](05-brand-marketing-proposals.md)), invoicing milestones, and GST/VAT/tax reserve planning.
- **Deliverables & Escalation:**
  - Financial Plan & Budget Summary, Unit Economics & Margin Scorecard, and Cost-Overrun Alerts escalated to **Team 1** and the **Developer** before irreversible spend.

## Team 13 — Marketing & Brand Team

**Scope:** Premium, memorable brand building, customer psychology, search visibility, and lead/sales generation.

- **Brand Identity & Strategy:**
  - Discovery & research, strategy & concept building, identity development (optionally using `Realtime-color` for palette/typography tokens and `Haikei` for SVG brand assets), brand applications & presentations, brand guideline revisions.
  - Customer psychology & emotional impression, brand voice/communications, consistent brand identity, market value, **packaging**, client feedback loops, and **terms & conditions**.
- **Search & Content Engine:**
  - Keyword research across **SEO** (Search Engine Optimization), **AEO** (Answer Engine Optimization), and **GEO** (Generative Engine Optimization).
  - Content Writer, poster/ad creative briefs, landing page & campaign copy.
- **CRM, Outreach & Sales Generation:**
  - Lead management & CRM workflows.
  - Channel-ready messaging & campaign templates for **WhatsApp** (optionally integrated via self-hosted `OpenWA` / `open-wa` after Team 6 vetting), **Facebook**, **Instagram**, and **LinkedIn** (human-approved before sending).
- **Proposal Templates:** Use [05-brand-marketing-proposals.md](05-brand-marketing-proposals.md) for client discovery, brand proposals, and 5-stage payment breakdowns.
