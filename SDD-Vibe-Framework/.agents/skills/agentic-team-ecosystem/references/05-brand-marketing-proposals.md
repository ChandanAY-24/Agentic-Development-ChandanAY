# 05 — Brand & Marketing Proposals, Discovery Prompts & Research Playbook

Use these canonical templates whenever **Team 13 (Marketing & Brand)** and **Team 3 (Frontend Design)** are activated for a Brand, Product, SaaS, or E-commerce engagement.

---

## 1. Client Discovery & Positioning Prompts

### Prompt A — Scope & Deliverables Intake
> "Thanks for sharing the brief. Before I suggest the price, I would like to understand the brand/product, its audience requirements, and the deliverables you need. Based on that, I can recommend the right approach to define the scope of the project."

### Prompt B — Holistic Brand Identity Positioning
> "I would approach this project as a complete brand identity, not just a logo design. We will first understand the brand positioning and its audience, and then develop visual directions that build a consistent identity system that works across packaging, digital platforms, and marketing."

---

## 2. 5-Phase Brand Identity Proposal Structure

1. **Discovery:** Brand, Target Audience, Business Goal.
2. **Brand Strategy:** Positioning, Personality, Differentiators.
3. **Core Brand Identity:** Logo, Typography, Color Palette, Brand Voice.
4. **Brand Applications:** Approved Identity, Packaging, Social Media, Product Mockups.
5. **Brand Guideline & Delivery:** Brand Guideline Document, Ready-to-Use Assets, Cross-Channel Consistency.

## 4. Brand Research & Definition Checklist

### Research Questions (Must Answer Before Design)
- Designed for **which audience**? (Demographics, psychographics, buying triggers)
- **Why is this brand relevant** for customers right now?
- For this category of brands, **who owns the market positions** today?
- **If the brand were one person**, what is its identity (tone, values, demeanor)?
- **Why are customers choosing us** across different touchpoints (web, packaging, support, social)?

### Brand Definition Outputs
- **Brand Problem:** The core perception, trust, or differentiation gap being solved.
- **Brand Concept:** The central creative/strategic idea unifying visuals and messaging.
- **Brand Insight:** The human/customer truth that drives emotional resonance and loyalty.
